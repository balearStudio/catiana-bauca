# Reference board — Clínica Dental Catiana Bauçà

Captured 2026-09-20. Screenshots in `refs/`. Each reference carries **what to take** — a
reference without that note is close to useless to the next stage. Nothing here is copied:
these set the quality bar and tell us what the sector's good work actually does.

---

## 1. `identalinca.com` — Institut Dental Inca · **Awwwards-listed** · `refs/idi-inca/`

**The most important reference on this board, because it is four minutes' walk away.**
A dental institute in the *same town* as our client, with an award-listed site.

- **Typeface:** Neue Montreal. One family, weights 100 / 400 / 700. No pairing.
- **Look:** black on white. Very large bold sentence-case headlines, Spanish (not Catalan).
- **Device:** line-drawn illustrations set *inline inside the headline* — a scaler, two
  figures in coats — so the sentence carries the pictures. Distinctive and cheap.
- **Also:** three grey cards (Tratamientos / Equipo / La Clínica) with `→` links. Note that
  an award-listed site still reaches for the pattern our checklist bans — the cards work
  there because each holds a real photograph, not because the pattern is good.

**What to take:** the confidence of one family at one big size, and the discipline of a
single clean grotesque. **What to avoid: everything else.** Our client must not be
black-on-white with a big bold grotesque, or she reads as the weaker copy of the practice
down the street. This reference is mostly here to tell us what *not* to be.

## 2. `aventuradentalarts.com` — Awwwards SOTD (Mar 2026) · `refs/aventura/`

- **Typeface:** Instrument Serif (display) + Inter Tight (text).
- **Look:** warm champagne neutrals, deliberately "de-medicalised" — reads as a boutique
  spa rather than a clinic.

**What to take:** the strategy of refusing the clinical register entirely. Confirms that a
display serif *can* win here — but it is the one direction our local competitor's neighbours
already occupy, and it fights our client's own hard-edged signage.

## 3. `halodental.com` — Awwwards SOTD · `refs/halo/`

- **Typeface:** Nuckle. Again **one family**, weights 400–700.
- **Look:** cream and true black in large flat fields.

**What to take:** the single-family discipline, twice confirmed, and the courage of big
flat colour fields with nothing in them.

## 4. Owner-supplied: *Cabinet dentaire Gonin Hetzel*

- **Look:** deep indigo/navy field, white clean grotesque, the practice name set very large,
  real team photography overlapping the type at different depths, numbered navigation
  (`00. 01. 02.`), phone number as a pill button top-right.

**What to take:** **the navy field.** It proves a dental site can hold a deep colour and
look more expensive for it, not more clinical. Also: the name set large and plainly, and
photographs of actual staff overlapped rather than placed in boxes.

## 5. Owner-supplied: *Soma* dental brand identity

- **Look:** mint + salmon + navy, rounded geometric sans, rounded cards.

**What to take:** the palette's nerve — three colours committed to, one of them warm.
**What to avoid:** the rounded-everything softness and the stock smiling dentist; this is
brand-guideline work, not a website, and it tips into the generic dental register.

## 6. Owner-supplied: *Dr. Henry Courtneyo*

- **Look:** the surname set enormous across the full width in heavy black grotesque, white
  page, hero photo with information cards overlapping its corners, a row of small-caps
  labels above the name.

**What to take:** a person's name at full page width as the main graphic event — relevant,
because our client's patients say *her name*, not the clinic's. **What to avoid:** the
stat strips ("10 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE", "17 AWWARDS WINNING") — our brief has no such
numbers and inventing them is the exact failure we already committed once.

pale blue. Enhanced for screen: the black becomes a **navy** that can carry a whole page,
the green brightens to a usable accent, the blue stays as a secondary.

## What the board concludes

1. **One grotesque family, used across a wide weight range.** Two of three award-winning
   references do exactly this, and neither pairs a serif with a sans. My earlier picks
   (Fraunces + Karla, Bricolage + Literata) were chosen for *not being banned* rather than
   from evidence, and they do not appear in work of this standard.
2. **A colour field, not a white page.** Both the navy reference and the client's own black
   fascia point the same way, and it separates her from the competitor in her own town.
3. **Real photographs of real people**, overlapped with the type rather than boxed.
4. **The palette is already hers.** It only needed sampling.
