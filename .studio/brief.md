# Research brief: Clínica Dental Catiana Bauçà

## 1. Who they are
A single-dentist dental clinic in **Inca**, the market town at the centre of Mallorca, run by **Dra. Catiana Bauçà**. Patients are local and long-term, not tourists: reviews repeatedly mention six years, "hace muchos años", whole families. What people actually praise is not technology but *vocación* — being treated as a person, and specifically being helped past fear of the dentist. The clinic is small, ground-floor, on an arcaded shopping street.

## 2. Services & offer
From the clinic's own window lettering (in Catalan) and directories: **conservadora, odontopediatria, periodòncia, ortodòncia, estètica, cirurgia i implants, pròtesi, ATM** (jaw joint / bruxism). Confirmed in practice from their own photos: digital **3D intraoral scanning** used to show patients their own mouth on screen, **GBT/Airflow** cleaning for sensitive patients, and **whitening**. No prices are published anywhere.

## 3. Reputation
Google: **4.8 from 68 reviews** (API returns only 5). Directory listings are stale and disagree — 4.6/43, 5.0/17, 4.8/10 — so use the Google figure. No complaints in what's retrievable, no press, no awards.

Recurring themes: overcoming fear, the dentist's personal manner, long-term loyalty, implant and reconstruction work, and the new sensitivity-friendly cleaning machine.

## 4. Tone of voice
Warm, plain, personal, first-person. Patients write about relief and trust, not about clinical excellence. Spanish `usted`/`tú` is mixed but leans informal. The clinic's own signage is **Catalan**; every retrievable review is in **Spanish** — both languages are genuinely in use.

## 6. Visual identity today
- **Logo:** a "CB" monogram inside a circle, drawn in thin teal/cyan line. Appears on the fascia, the window, the reception desk and the wall.
- **Colours:** teal/cyan primary, with blue and green stripes on the signage; staff scrubs are the same teal. Interior is white walls, pale wood floor, one green horizontal line on the wall.
- **No website, no official Instagram** (the dentist has a personal account), a personal Facebook profile only. There is no existing web design to improve on — this is a first site.
- **Imagery:** 10 photos, all from the Google Business profile, now downloaded to `sources/images/`. They are **phone snapshots, not professional photography** — honest and warm, but uneven. Best of them: a hygienist showing a reclined patient their 3D scan on screen; a whitening session under the blue lamp with a **beach video playing on the ceiling-mounted screen**; the daylight storefront.

## 8. GAPS & LOW-CONFIDENCE
**Blocking**
- None, but see imagery below — it constrains the design more than anything else.

**Useful**
- **Photography is the single biggest upgrade available.** Everything we have is a snapshot from a phone. A half-day shoot of the dentist, the team, the rooms and two treatments would change the result more than any design decision.
- **Consent:** identifiable patients appear in three photos, and one (a man smiling up from the chair) looks patient-submitted rather than clinic-owned. Do not publish any of them without written permission.
- **Team unknown.** At least two other people appear in the photos; no names, roles or count. Reviews credit "todo el personal".
- **Dra. Bauçà's background** — training, years practising, why she opened in Inca — is nowhere public, and is exactly what this brief's voice says patients care about.
- **No prices** published, and no email address.

**Low-confidence**
- Opening hours (see above). Review counts differ wildly between directories; trust Google.
- The Catalan/Spanish split is inferred from signage vs reviews, not from the client.
